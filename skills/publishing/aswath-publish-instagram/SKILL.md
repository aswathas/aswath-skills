# Publish to Instagram

Path on this machine: the **`everyhand-instagram` MCP** (`ig_*` tools). It is
registered in `~/.claude.json` and exposes `ig_publish`.

## Before any publish

1. `ig_status` — check `connection`. If it says `logged_out`, stop: tell the
   user the Instagram session expired and that they must log in through the
   EveryHand app. After they do, run `ig_daemon action=reload` and re-check
   `ig_status` before posting. Never post into a logged-out session.
2. `ig_account action=info` — confirm the account identity you are posting to.
3. Check the media file exists and is inside an allowed folder.

## Publishing

`ig_publish(kind, path, paths, caption, thumbnail, schedule_at, usertags,
location, mentions, links, hashtags, music_query, music_id, confirm)`

| `kind` | Media argument |
|---|---|
| `photo` | `path` (single image) |
| `video` | `path` |
| `reel` | `path` |
| `album` | `paths` — 2 to 10 images |
| `story_photo` / `story_video` | `path` |

Extras:

- `caption` — the text. `hashtags` are **stories only**; `mentions` and `links`
  are http URLs.
- `location` — a place name (first search hit wins) or a numeric place id.
- `usertags` — usernames, for feed kinds only.
- `schedule_at` — ISO time with its UTC offset, 10 minutes to 75 days ahead.
  Photo, album and video only.
- `music_query` / `music_id` — photo, album and reel only. Story music needs
  MoviePy, which is not installed here. Find a track with `ig_discover kind=music`.
- `thumbnail` — optional; videos get one generated by ffmpeg when omitted.
  `.heic` photos are converted to `.jpg` automatically.

Allowed media roots: `store/media`, `temp`, `Pictures`, `Videos`, `Downloads`,
`Desktop`, `Music`, or anything in `IG_UPLOAD_DIRS`. A path outside them fails.

## Two-step confirm — mandatory

1. The first call changes nothing. It returns `needs_confirm` plus a preview
   and puts a 6-digit code on the account owner's screen.
2. Show the owner the preview. Only after they say yes, call again with the
   **same arguments** plus `confirm=<the 6-digit code>`. Single use, 6 minutes,
   never guess it. If the owner declines, the tool reports it — do not retry.

## Limits

- Max 3 publishes per minute, 10 per hour, 25 per day.
- The same publish (same files, caption, options) within 10 minutes returns the
  first result with `duplicate: true`. That is not a failure — never re-post it.
- A 429 with `cooldown_until` means Instagram blocked the action. Wait for the
  cooldown. Do not retry.

## Reading and cleanup

- `ig_media`, `ig_user_media`, `ig_comments`, `ig_relationship`,
  `ig_followers`, `ig_discover` — read-only inspection.
- `ig_daemon action=status|restart|stop|reload` — `restart` is the self-heal for
  a wedged daemon; `reload` re-reads the session after a fresh login.

## Rules

- Never post without an explicit go-ahead on the exact caption, media and kind.
  Instagram posts can be deleted but not cleanly taken back.
- Do not auto-publish to a business/creator account's insights or edit the
  profile without being asked.
- A reel is a video file; check duration and aspect before posting.
